About HireFlow

Making career discovery simpler.

HireFlow is a student-built recruitment platform concept designed to connect candidates and employers through a clear, modern experience.

Our mission

Turn job searching into a focused journey.

HireFlow brings job discovery, company exploration and application tracking together in one place.

Project objectives

  • Create an intuitive job search interface.
  • Present opportunities in a structured format.
  • Provide a responsive experience across devices.
  • Demonstrate modern HTML5 and CSS3 skills.
